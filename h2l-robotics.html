Robotics Development, Integration and Field Commissioning

Summary

At H2L Robotics, I worked on the development, commissioning and field testing of agricultural robotic systems. My role combined hands-on mechanical work with mechatronic troubleshooting, hardware integration and collaboration with the engineering team. I contributed to commissioning a new generation of robotic systems in Italy and independently installed and tested a new oil-spraying subsystem on three tulip selection robots in New Zealand.

  • Role: Mechatronics Service Engineer
  • Focus: Hardware integration, commissioning, prototyping and troubleshooting
  • Locations: Netherlands, Italy and New Zealand
  • Duration: February – October 2026
3 Robots commissioned with the new subsystem
100+ Growers supported
3 Countries of field experience

Subsystem Installation and Testing – New Zealand

I independently travelled to New Zealand to install and commission a new oil-spraying subsystem on three tulip selection robots. The work involved integrating the new hardware into the existing robotic systems, checking the installation and testing its operation on-site.

The installation and testing required working directly with the existing machine hardware and verifying that the new subsystem operated as intended within the complete robotic system.

Commissioning – Italy

Together with a colleague, I contributed to commissioning a new generation of agricultural robotic systems at a customer site in Italy. This involved working directly with the machines during installation and initial testing to support their deployment.

Prototyping and Field Troubleshooting

Alongside commissioning, I supported product development and field operations through mechanical prototyping, test setups and mechatronic troubleshooting. I worked on issues involving mechanical components, electronics, sensors and software, often diagnosing problems directly on customer machines.

  • Designed and built mechanical prototypes and test setups.
  • Performed hardware verification and functional testing.
  • Diagnosed mechanical, electrical and sensor-related faults.
  • Worked with customers and communicated technical findings to the engineering team.
